🏆 My Stickers

For grown-ups 💜

  • This game teaches phonics: letters make sounds ("mmm", not "em"). Encourage your child to say each sound out loud along with the game.
  • The toggles on the first screen choose the word mix: 3-letter words (60 of them), easy 4-letter words, and sight words. Start with 3-letter only; add the others as your child grows. Short 5–10 minute sessions work best.
  • How to play: read the big word, then tap the matching picture. The 🐌 button sounds the word out slowly. Sight words (common words like "the" and "who") usually have no picture and can't be sounded out — so in those rounds the game says the word aloud (tap 🔊 to hear it again) and your child finds the written word. The 🐌 button spells them letter by letter.
  • Vowels are orange, consonants are blue — this helps kids spot the vowel pattern in the middle of each word.
  • Every 3 ⭐ earns a sticker in the sticker book!
  • Works completely offline. The voice uses your device's built-in speech — if it's silent, turn up the volume (and on iPads, check the mute switch). Stars reset when the page is closed.
Voice:

Voices come from this device — pick whichever sounds nicest. Ones marked 🌐 need internet; the rest work offline. Tip: for a much more human voice on iPad/iPhone/Mac, install an "Enhanced" or "Premium" voice under Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices, then reopen this page.
